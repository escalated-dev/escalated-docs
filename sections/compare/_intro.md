# Compare Escalated

Escalated is an open-source, embeddable helpdesk that lives inside your existing application. Unlike standalone support platforms that require customers to leave your app, Escalated integrates directly into your product across multiple frameworks — Laravel, Rails, Django, AdonisJS, Symfony, NestJS, Phoenix, Go, .NET, Spring, and WordPress. Filament provides a native Laravel admin interface.

This section compares Escalated to popular helpdesk and customer support platforms so you can make an informed decision.

## Why teams choose Escalated

**You own your data.** Escalated runs on your servers. You choose storage, email providers, and external integrations; those integrations can transmit data outside your servers.

**No per-agent fees.** Add as many agents as you need without worrying about ballooning costs. Escalated is free and open source.

**It lives in your app.** Customers get support without leaving your product. Agents work in the same environment your team already uses.

**Works with your stack.** Whether you're running Laravel, Rails, Django, AdonisJS, WordPress, or Filament, Escalated has a native adapter. Most web adapters use the shared Inertia Vue interface. WordPress and Filament use native interfaces.

**Extend with plugins.** Install plugins compatible with your backend and review their provider and runtime requirements. Source availability does not guarantee a released integration or identical behavior across frameworks.
