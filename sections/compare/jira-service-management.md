# Escalated vs Jira Service Management

Jira Service Management Cloud focuses on IT service management in the Atlassian ecosystem. Escalated is an MIT-licensed customer support package installed inside your application.

## Feature comparison

| Dimension | Escalated | Jira Service Management Cloud |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Ticket conversations | Core package | Included in the support product |
| Hosting and administration | Your host application and infrastructure | Vendor-managed service |
| Core licence fees | No per-agent licence fees | Plan and usage terms apply |

Jira Service Management is part of Atlassian Service Collection. It provides request workflows, queues, a knowledge base, incident handling, and asset/configuration management on applicable plans.

Plugins have separate installation and provider requirements. Channel and AI integrations are not a promise of identical behavior on every backend. Hosting, email, storage, provider usage, and maintenance still cost money with Escalated. A compliance plugin does not certify a deployment as HIPAA-compliant or SOC 2-audited.

## Key differentiators

- **Open source and self-hosted.** Escalated core is MIT-licensed and can run on your own infrastructure.
- **Embeds into your application.** Configure the backend adapter with your authentication, storage, and deployment. WordPress and Filament have native interfaces; the other supported web adapters use the shared Vue interface.
- **No core per-agent pricing.** Agent count does not change the core licence fee. Compare operating costs and the features your team actually needs.

## Pricing comparison

The [Jira Service Management pricing page](https://www.atlassian.com/collections/service/pricing?bundle=jira-service-management) lists the following terms. Amounts, where shown, are USD before tax.

| Plan | Published terms |
|------|-----------------|
| Free | Three agents, $0 |
| Paid plans | Use the vendor calculator for team size and billing cycle |

Paid prices depend on agent count and billing period. Use the current calculator rather than multiplying an old flat rate across every team size.

Use Jira Service Management when IT service management, incidents, changes, or asset management determine the purchase. Escalated is a customer support package and does not provide an ITSM suite.

*Prices and requirements as of November 2026.*
